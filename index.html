One Ear Up Publisher

This is a small personal automation tool that publishes AI-generated golden retriever short videos to a single TikTok account that the developer owns and operates. It is not a public service and does not act on behalf of, or accept connections from, any other TikTok account.

The videos are produced with an AI video generation pipeline (visual continuity maintained via a fixed reference character) and published automatically after a manual human review step.
